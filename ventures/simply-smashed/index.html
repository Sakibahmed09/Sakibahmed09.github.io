We did not plan any of this. The pandemic shut my dad's restaurant, my parents were stranded in Bangladesh, and me and my brother had a kitchen and nothing to cook in it.

Sakib Ahmed Co-founder of Draper · Own your distribution · 7 Nov 2024 in

I didn’t plan to build Simply Smashed – we built it to survive. In early 2020, just as the lockdown was being announced, my parents boarded a flight back to Bangladesh, leaving my brother, Sifat Ahmed, and me to manage our family’s Italian restaurant in East London. What was meant to be a routine trip turned into a months-long ordeal. Suddenly, we were on our own, struggling to keep the business afloat without knowing when our parents could return. Those months were some of the most intense and challenging of my life. Between finishing my degree apprenticeship at Goldman Sachs, navigating repatriation flights to bring my parents, and even dealing with a burglary at the restaurant, it felt like we were up against a storm. We knew we had to go back to basics. We transformed the delivery experience, launched two critical campaigns, and even got featured on Islam Channel for our efforts to donate pizzas to NHS staff during the pandemic – all while operating from the red. This is why community is one of the foundations of our brand. Simply Smashed was born out of sheer necessity. It wasn’t a start from scratch; it was a start from survival. Today, we're proud to be a family-run business as the highest rated burger joint in London (4.9* 1800+ Google reviews) with a story grounded in resilience and community. Loved speaking to Areeb Siddiqui on his podcast about our journey and what's to come next!

I keep entering domains the slow way in the fastest era in history. Twice now and I'd do it again. You can go to Claude right now and start building in a domain you have zero expertise in. And it might work tbh, in fact, we're in the most competitive era of software in history because of that. So why would you go into a domain you haven't earned? Go into the one where you've put the most reps in. That's your edge now. We grew Simply Smashed to the highest rated burger in London organically and that whole experience pulled me into writing for founders at agencies. I did that for a year, which led to Draper. With Vibenasheeds same thing. Grew a YouTube channel first, figured out what hit and what didn't, then started building. It reminds me of something Abdel once said to me... AI gets you 60% of the way there, it's on you to fill the gap. That 60% is everything that's ever been publicly scraped. The other 40% doesn't exist in any dataset. You learn it in the arena.
