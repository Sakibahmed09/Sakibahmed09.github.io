What I say about it now, and what I was actually posting at the time.

A side-quest that got out of hand in the good way. Vocals only, no instruments, made because I wanted something I could actually put on.

The peak was a mate playing them in the background at his wedding. Nothing I have shipped since has felt quite like that.

Then I stopped. It had been a fun ride and I had run out of things to say in it.
