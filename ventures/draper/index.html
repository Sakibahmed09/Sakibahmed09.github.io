What I say about it now, and what I was actually posting at the time.

For the last two years of the restaurant I was quietly advising Series A founders on their content on the side. Nobody knew. I was taking calls with YC founders between burger orders.

People keep asking why I left food for this. The honest answer is that the restaurant taught me more about building than anything else I have done, and then I wanted to build the thing that scaled it.

The thesis I keep repeating, to the point that Mustafa has told me to stop. We ran a burger joint to the top of the country on zero paid marketing by documenting the mess of building it. That is not marketing attached to a product. That is the product.

Most AI content is slop, and everyone can feel it while scrolling even if they cannot name why. The point of AI here is to sound more like yourself, not less.

Mustafa brought a deep work system over from his old agency. I thought it was rigid. It doubled our focus time without adding an hour to the week and it cost nothing.

On the division of labour, there is not much to add.

You can open Claude right now and start building in a field you know nothing about, which is exactly why I keep entering domains the slow way. Twice now, and I would do it again.
