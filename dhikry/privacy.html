privacy policy

last updated: september 6, 2026

dhikry supports daily Islamic remembrance and private circles. this policy explains the data used for your account, social features, usage analytics and diagnostics.

what never leaves your device

  • your location. if you grant location access, your GPS coordinates are used to calculate prayer times and are stored only on your device. coordinates are never sent to our servers. (only your timezone name, e.g. "Europe/London", is stored server-side for notification timing.)
  • your contacts. if you grant contacts access, dhikry reads first names and phone numbers on-device to suggest people to pray for and invite. your contact list is never uploaded anywhere. invites are sent by you, from your own Messages app or share sheet. we only pre-fill the message text.
  • share cards. milestone images are rendered on-device and saved to your photo library only when you ask.

what we do not do

  • we do not sell or rent your data. providers listed below process data to operate the app, and circle members see the social information described above
  • we do not show ads or use advertising trackers
  • we do not track you across other apps or websites
  • we do not upload your contacts or your location
  • we do not use HealthKit or collect fitness measurements
  • product usage events are stored in our own database; Sentry processes diagnostics as described above

guest mode

you can use dhikry without an account ("skip · save later"). in guest mode your streak and session progress live on your device. the usage analytics and diagnostics described above may still be sent. social features with real people require an account. daily 100 counts are stored on the device and are separate from shared session completion.

where your data is stored

account and circle data are stored with Supabase (our database and authentication provider), protected by row-level security so users can only read what they are meant to see. crash reports, when enabled, go to Sentry. both act as data processors on our behalf.

notifications

notifications are opt-in. daily reminders are scheduled on your device; circle notifications (e.g. a gentle nudge from a circle member) are delivered through apple's push service using your push token. you can turn any of this off in settings or in iOS settings at any time.

sharing your in-progress session

check settings → "share when I’m praying" to see or change your current preference. sharing is enabled by default for the current early-access rollout. when enabled, people who share a circle can see that your morning or evening session is in progress. blocked users cannot see each other's presence. when updates stop, visible presence expires after a short timeout; expired presence records are retained temporarily to prevent delayed network requests from restarting an ended session.

children

dhikry is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

your rights & deleting your data

you can sign out at any time (this removes your push token from our servers).

delete your account in the app

open settings → delete account. after you confirm, deletion is immediate and permanent. it removes, on our servers:

  • your profile and sign-in identity, including the email held for that account
  • your sessions, streaks, and circle memberships
  • your dua requests, the duas you received, and any unsent duas
  • your push token, notification history, and membership notices
  • your presence preferences and session-presence records

two things, for your circle's sake: duas you already sent stay visible to the people who received them, but your name is removed from them. and if you created a circle that still has other members, the circle continues under its longest-standing member. your departure does not delete your friends' circle.

installation-level analytics and diagnostics are stored separately from your account and are not automatically erased by this account-deletion operation. contact us for help with a data-access or deletion request that includes those records.

if you used Sign in with Apple, deleting your Dhikry account does not automatically remove Apple’s remaining sign-in connection. after deletion, the app shows how to remove it in Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → Dhikry. Apple’s instructions. your Dhikry account deletion does not depend on completing this extra step.
