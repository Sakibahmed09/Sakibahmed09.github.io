frequently asked

i don't have an invite code. how do i get in?

you do not need an invite to start. choose "start your circle" on the welcome screen, or use "skip · save later" at sign-in to practise on your own. to join friends, ask for their circle's 6-character code.

my invite code says it doesn't match

codes are 6 characters, letters and numbers only — no spaces. they're not case-sensitive. if the code looks right but still won't validate, the circle may be full (circles hold up to 12 people) — ask your friend to check, or email us.

what is a circle?

a circle is a private group of up to 12 people who keep up their adhkar together. you can belong to multiple circles, with separate groups for family, friends and others. members of one circle cannot see a different circle unless they also belong to it.

how do i invite someone to my circle?

open your circle screen and share your code — the share button pre-fills a message you can send by text or WhatsApp. when they enter your code on their welcome screen, they're in.

how do i leave a circle?

open "your circles", tap the circle's menu and choose "leave circle". creators can leave too: another member takes over if people remain. leaving does not delete your account or personal streak. the circle closes when its last member leaves.

notifications aren't arriving

check iOS settings → notifications → dhikry is allowed, then check the in-app settings screen for your reminder preferences. reminders are scheduled in your local timezone. if you signed out and back in, open the app once so it can re-register for notifications.

how do streaks work?

your personal streak grows when you complete an adhkar session on consecutive local days. missing a day breaks the run. the daily 100 counters are separate: they do not mark a morning or evening session complete.

does dhikry upload my contacts or location?

no. contacts are read on your device only, to suggest people to pray for — they're never uploaded. location is used on-device to calculate prayer times. details in our privacy policy.

how do i delete my account?

open settings → delete account and confirm. this permanently removes your account and its associated data; signing out alone does not delete it. duas already received by other people remain without your name, and circles with other members continue under another owner. if you cannot access the app, email sakib@withsignal.io for help.
