dhikr is better together

keep up your morning and evening adhkar with the people you care about. start on your own, create a circle, or join your friends.

circles, not feeds

create separate circles for family and friends, or join with their 6-character code. see who has finished their adhkar and follow your group's progress.

morning & evening adhkar

read Arabic, transliteration and English meanings with sources included. choose your session length, count at your own pace and return to unfinished progress.

duas by name

make dua for a friend and send it to them. when a dua arrives for you, say ameen to reveal who sent it. you can make dua for them back.
