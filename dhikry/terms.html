About Dhikry

Dhikry is a daily adhkar (Islamic remembrance) app that connects you with a small circle of friends for mutual prayer and accountability.

Your account

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account. You must be 13 years or older to use Dhikry.

Acceptable use

Your display name, circle names and any written content are visible to other people. We have no tolerance for objectionable content or for abusive behaviour towards anyone using Dhikry.

  • Use Dhikry for its intended purpose: daily Islamic remembrance and prayer
  • Do not use names, written content, duas or invitations to send harmful, offensive or inappropriate material
  • Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, or abuse anyone in your circle
  • Do not post anything hateful, discriminatory, sexual, violent, or otherwise objectionable
  • Do not attempt to access duas or circles you are not a member of
  • Do not spam or abuse the nudge or invite features

Reporting and blocking

To report or block a circle member, open their profile from a circle and tap the visible menu in the top right. You do not have to receive a dua from them first. Blocking prevents their duas from reaching you and hides in-progress session presence between you. It does not remove either person from a shared circle. A connection is needed to save the block; the app tells you if it could not save it.

We review every report within 24 hours. Content we find to be objectionable is removed, and accounts that post objectionable content or behave abusively are removed from Dhikry. You can also report anything to sakib@withsignal.io.

Ending your access

We may suspend or remove any account that breaks these terms, without notice where the content or behaviour warrants it.

Content

Guided adhkar include source references. The short English prayers you can send to friends are original supplications, not presented as quotations from the Quran or hadith.
