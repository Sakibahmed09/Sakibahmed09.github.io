How this site works

16 August 2026

I saw benji.org and couldn’t leave it alone. This is my version of that discipline, rebuilt from a blank file: paper, ink, and one red biro.

Type Colour Motion Small details Colophon

01 Type

Four typefaces, none of them costing a penny. I tried a Caslon first because it had a good story attached, then looked at it next to the sans and binbagged it. A story is not a reason.

Schibsted Grotesk carries the prose.

built for a Scandinavian newsroom, reads like one

Garamond leans in when a word matters.

its italic comes from chancery hands, so emphasis looks handwritten

and the margin notes are an actual ballpoint

Reenie Beanie. the one red biro is a real biro

سبحان الله

Amiri counts the dhikr. Revived from the Amiria Press in Cairo, which is where I went quiet for two years

02 Colour

Warm paper, warm ink, and a single seal red spent like money. If everything is highlighted, nothing is. The only gold in the house appears when you select text, so go on, select this sentence.

one accent, spent carefully

Dark mode is called after Maghrib in the stylesheet. The moon in the footer is the same sun with its rays folded away, and the site remembers which one you left on.

03 Motion

Three speeds, named like gears. Swift at 160ms for feedback, settle at 340ms for state, grand at 700ms for entrances. Everything decelerates like a real object and nothing bounces, except the tasbih bead, which has earned it.

tap to race the three gears
swift 160
settle 340
grand 700

If your system asks for reduced motion, this site sits still. That includes the race you just ran.

04 Small details

The clock in the footer keeps London time wherever you read this from, because that’s where the kettle is. Press ⌘K anywhere for the command palette. Press t for the tasbih: thirty-three counts complete the ring, and the phrase changes each round, the way it should.

try t. go on.

The favicon is a seal: س stamped in red, the first letter of my name. The margin notes draw themselves in as you reach them, ink first, words after, the way notes actually get written. Text you select turns gold. Links underline in hairline ink and only commit to red when you hover, which is roughly my approach to commitment in general (ask Mustafa).

05 Colophon

No framework, no analytics, nothing to install. Nine pages, one stylesheet, one script. The venture pages get generated by a small Python script that reads my own archive, so what actually loads in your browser is still just static files. Heaviest page on the site is 215KB and most of that is the fonts.

Written and built with Claude Code in one long sitting, art directed by me. The format owes a debt to Benji Taylor, the taste bar he set, and the hour I spent reading his CSS like tafsir.

If you want one of these for yourself, you know where the agency is.
