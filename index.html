I was born in Dièppe, France, moved to east London at eight. Trained as an engineer and started at Goldman Sachs at eighteen, placed on the wrong team.

These days I’m a co-founder at Draper with Mustafa and Aaquib, where we turn founders into industry authorities. Mustafa and I met at an agency and spent two years there on YC founders and a16z-backed companies before we went and did our own version of it. The thesis hasn’t moved since, that content is product and product is content, and Mustafa has asked me to stop saying it.

Before Draper I built Simply Smashed with my brother, out of the back of a flower shop. London’s highest rated burger joint on Google. 4.9 stars across 3,000 reviews, and not a pound of paid marketing behind any of it. He runs it now and runs it better, which I get told regularly.

I also built MiniDeed as a student, a social app where likes became penny donations. It raised £26k for charity across 25 countries and still takes donations daily.

On the side I build small Muslim software, things like dhikry and vibenasheeds and Bilal. I’m a tinkerer more than anything.

Find me on X and LinkedIn, or by email.

AI has scraped nearly the entire internet. It still can't touch the one thing that matters, and it's sat between your ears. Here's what keeps me up, and I run an AI content startup so this should be the last thing I'd say. Every tool works the same way. Dead internet, live, and my own industry flooring it. You can copy the point but not the vector. You can copy where someone landed. You can't copy the mind that keeps getting them there. That's the whole reason Draper doesn't just scrape the internet. It scrapes you. The calls, the thing you've said out loud fifty times and never thought to write down. So what goes back out is actually new. Not more slop on the pile, it's you, amplified.
