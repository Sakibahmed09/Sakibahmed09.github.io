Sakib Ahmed

Updated 16 August 2026

I was born in Dièppe, a small town in northern France with barely a halal takeaway in it. We moved to Tower Hamlets when I was eight, which solved that problem permanently. French is still my first language.

I trained as an engineer and started at Goldman Sachs at eighteen, first cohort of the apprenticeship, placed on the wrong team. There was a basement prayer room. I used it a lot.

As a student I co-founded MiniDeed, a social app where likes became penny donations. £26k raised across 25 countries and £0 in revenue. We were very consistent about the £0. It still takes donations daily, which beats any MRR I’ve had since.

In 2021 I went quiet. Two years in Egypt studying Qur’an and Arabic, zero tweets. I came back and built Simply Smashed with my brother, from the back of a flower shop. Highest rated burger joint in the UK. 4.9 stars, 3,000 reviews, no paid marketing ever. He runs it now and runs it better, which I get told regularly.

These days I’m a co-founder at Draper with Mustafa and Aaquib. We turn founders into industry authorities. I lead product. The thesis hasn’t moved in two years: content is product, and product is content. Mustafa has asked me to stop saying it.

On the side I build small Muslim software. dhikry, for dhikr with my closest circle. vibenasheeds, which writes music and then strips the music back out. Bilal, which puts masjid times on my living room TV. No business case for any of them man. You can just do things.
