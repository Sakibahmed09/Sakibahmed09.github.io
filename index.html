Updated 16 August 2026

I was born in Dièppe, moved to Tower Hamlets at eight. Trained as an engineer and started at Goldman Sachs at eighteen, placed on the wrong team. There was a basement prayer room. I used it a lot.

These days I’m a co-founder at Draper with Mustafa and Aaquib. We turn founders into industry authorities. The thesis hasn’t moved in two years: content is product, and product is content. Mustafa has asked me to stop saying it.

Before that, MiniDeed as a student: £26k raised for charity, £0 in revenue. We were very consistent about the £0. Then Simply Smashed with my brother, out of the back of a flower shop, London’s highest rated burger joint on Google. He runs it now and runs it better, which I get told regularly.

On the side I build small Muslim software. dhikry, vibenasheeds, Bilal. I’m a tinkerer more than anything.

The long version is in the chapters. Otherwise find me on X and LinkedIn, or by email.
