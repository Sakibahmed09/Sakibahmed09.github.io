بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

Sakib Ahmed

Updated 16 August 2026

I was born in Dièppe, a small town in northern France with barely a halal takeaway in it. We moved to Tower Hamlets, East London when I was eight. French is still my first language.

I trained as an engineer and started out at Goldman Sachs. There was a basement prayer room. I used it a lot.

As a student I co-founded MiniDeed, a social app where likes became penny donations to charity. It reached 25 countries and raised £26k before we let it rest. Donations still tick through to this day.

In 2021 I went quiet. Two years in Egypt studying Qur’an and Arabic, zero tweets. I came back and built Simply Smashed with my brother, from the back of a flower shop. It became the highest rated burger joint in the UK. 4.9 stars, 3,000 reviews, no paid marketing ever. He runs it now, better than I did.

Today I’m a co-founder at Draper, where we turn founders into industry authorities. I lead product. The thesis is one I’ve been repeating for two years: content is product, and product is content.

On the side I build small Muslim software, like dhikry, a dhikr app for close friends, and vibenasheeds, halal music generation. You can just do things.

You can find me on X and LinkedIn, or reach me by email.
