Sakib Ahmed

Updated 16 August 2026

I was born in Dièppe, a small town in northern France with barely a halal takeaway in it. We moved to Tower Hamlets when I was eight. French is still my first language.

I trained as an engineer and started out at Goldman Sachs. There was a basement prayer room. I used it a lot.

As a student I co-founded MiniDeed, a social app where likes became penny donations. £26k across 25 countries, none of it to us. It still takes donations daily, which beats any MRR I’ve had since.

In 2021 I went quiet. Two years in Egypt studying Qur’an and Arabic, zero tweets. I came back and built Simply Smashed with my brother, from the back of a flower shop. Highest rated burger joint in the UK. 4.9 stars, 3,000 reviews, no paid marketing ever. He runs it now, better than I did.

These days I’m a co-founder at Draper with Mustafa and Aaquib. We turn founders into industry authorities. I lead product. The thesis hasn’t changed in two years: content is product, and product is content.

On the side I build small Muslim software. dhikry, dhikr with my closest circle. vibenasheeds, halal music generation. Bilal, masjid times on my living room TV. No business case for any of them. You can just do things.

Find me on X and LinkedIn, or reach me by email.
