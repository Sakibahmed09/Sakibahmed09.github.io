Updated 25 October 2026

I was born in Dieppe, France, and moved to east London when I was eight. At eighteen Goldman Sachs took me on as an apprentice engineer, first cohort, and put me on the wrong team. I stayed five years, and along the way I talked my way onto someone else’s side project, MiniDeed, an app where every like was a penny to charity. It raised £26k across 25 countries.

Then my brother and I built Simply Smashed out of the back of a flower shop. It became London’s highest rated burger joint on Google, 4.9 stars across 3,000 reviews, and we never spent a pound on paid marketing. We just told people what we were building. He runs it now and runs it better, which I get told regularly.

These days I’m building Draper with Mustafa and Aaquib, and we run LinkedIn for founders and their teams. So far that’s 400M+ views for 100+ founders, from YC and a16z-backed companies to VC funds, and north of £20M in pipeline from the content. Content is product and product is content. I’ve said it maybe 200 times now.

I also build apps for Muslims. Bilal is an athan that tells you when to leave for jama’ah at your masjid, not when the prayer starts. It’s on the iPhone, the Mac and the TV in your kitchen, and it knows over 24,000 masajid. There’s also dhikry, which is invite only, and vibenasheeds.

Find me on X and LinkedIn, or by email.
